About Rajgir

Rajgir is an ancient city located in Nalanda district of Bihar. It was the first capital of the Magadha Kingdom and played a significant role in Indian history.

Surrounded by beautiful hills, Rajgir is famous for its religious significance in Buddhism, Jainism, and Hinduism.

Historical Importance

Rajgir was closely associated with Lord Buddha, who spent many years here preaching his teachings. It was also an important center for Jainism.

The city was once ruled by King Bimbisara and later became an important part of the Mauryan Empire.

Main Attractions

Vishwa Shanti Stupa

Rajgir Ropeway

One of the oldest ropeways in India.

Griddhakuta Hill

Place where Lord Buddha delivered sermons.

Hot Water Springs

Popular religious and tourist attraction.

Cyclopean Wall

Ancient stone wall surrounding old Rajgir.

Jain Temples

Sacred pilgrimage destination for Jains.

Interesting Facts

  • Important Buddhist pilgrimage site.
  • Surrounded by five scenic hills.
  • Famous for hot water springs.
