Bihar Student Credit Card Scheme

Education Support Scheme for Bihar Students

About Bihar Student Credit Card Scheme

Bihar Student Credit Card Scheme was launched by the Government of Bihar under the Bihar Development Mission. The main objective of this scheme is to provide financial assistance to students who want to continue higher education but face financial problems.

Under this scheme, eligible students can get education loans for higher studies. The loan amount is provided through banks and helps students complete their professional and technical courses.

Objective of the Scheme

  • To increase higher education opportunities in Bihar
  • To provide financial support to students
  • To help students study in professional courses
  • To improve employment opportunities

Benefits of Student Credit Card

Education Loan

Bihar Student Credit Card Scheme provides financial support to students for higher education. Students can use this loan amount for college fees, course expenses, books, study materials, hostel fees and other educational requirements. This scheme helps students continue their studies without financial difficulties.

Loan Amount

Under this scheme, eligible students can get an education loan up to ₹4 lakh. The loan amount can be used for professional and higher education courses. It supports students in completing their graduation, technical education and career-oriented programs.

Low Interest

The Bihar government provides support to make education loans affordable for students. The low interest facility reduces the financial burden on students and their families. It helps students focus on their education and build a better future.

Career Support

This scheme helps students complete higher education and develop skills required for employment. By supporting professional courses, it creates better career opportunities and helps students achieve their goals.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be a resident of Bihar
  • Student should have passed 12th class
  • Admission should be taken in recognized institution
  • Age criteria should be fulfilled according to scheme rules
  • Student should not already have another education loan

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Residence Certificate
  • 12th Marksheet
  • Admission Proof
  • Bank Account Details
  • Passport Size Photo
  • Income Certificate

Courses Covered

  • Engineering
  • Medical Courses
  • Management Courses
  • Professional Courses
  • Technical Education
  • Higher Education Programs

Why This Scheme is Important?

Bihar Student Credit Card Scheme helps students achieve their education goals without financial burden. It supports talented students and creates better career opportunities.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much loan is available?

Eligible students can get an education loan up to ₹4 lakh for higher education and professional courses.

Who can apply?

Students who are residents of Bihar, have completed 12th class and havetaken admission in a recognized educational institution can apply.

Is online application available?

Yes, students can apply online through the official Bihar Student CreditCard portal.
