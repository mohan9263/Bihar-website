About Vaishali

Vaishali is one of the oldest republics in the world and an important historical city of Bihar. It is known for its association with both Buddhism and Jainism.

Vaishali was the birthplace of Lord Mahavira, the 24th Tirthankara of Jainism, and was frequently visited by Lord Buddha.

Historical Importance

Vaishali is often called the birthplace of democracy because it had a republican form of government more than 2,500 years ago.

Lord Buddha delivered his last sermon here before attaining Mahaparinirvana.
