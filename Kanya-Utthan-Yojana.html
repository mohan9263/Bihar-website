Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana

About Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana

Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana is a welfare scheme launched by the Bihar Government for the education and empowerment of girls. The main purpose of this scheme is to provide financial support to girls and encourage them to continue their studies. This scheme helps girls from birth to higher education by providing different benefits at various stages. It promotes women's education, health, independence and creates better opportunities for girls to build a successful future.

Objectives of the Scheme

The main objective of Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana is to increase the education level among girls and encourage them to complete school and higher education. The scheme provides financial assistance and creates better opportunities for girls to become independent, improve their skills and build a successful future.

Benefits of Kanya Utthan Yojana

Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana provides financial support to eligible girl students at different stages of education. This scheme helps girls continue their studies by reducing financial problems and supporting educational expenses. It encourages girls to complete higher education and develop skills for better career opportunities. The scheme also promotes women's empowerment, improves the social and economic condition of girls and helps them become self-dependent. Through this initiative, the Bihar Government aims to increase education awareness among girls and create equal opportunities for their future growth.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be a permanent resident of Bihar.
  • Applicant should be a girl student.
  • Student must be studying in a recognized institution.
  • Bank account should be available.
  • Required documents must be submitted.

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Residence Certificate
  • Bank Passbook
  • Educational Marksheet
  • Mobile Number
  • Passport Size Photo

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What is Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana?

Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana is a Bihar Government welfare scheme started to support the education and development of girls. The scheme provides financial assistance to eligible girls so that they can continue their studies and achieve their career goals.

Q2. Who can apply for Kanya Utthan Yojana?

Girls who are permanent residents of Bihar and studying in recognized educational institutions can apply for this scheme. Applicants must fulfill the eligibility conditions and provide required documents during the application process.

Q3. What benefits are provided under this scheme?

The scheme provides financial support for education and encourages girls to complete their studies. It helps reduce financial burden on families, promotes higher education and supports girls in becoming independent.

Q4. Which documents are required for application?

Applicants need documents like Aadhaar Card, Residence Certificate, Bank Account Details, Educational Marksheet, Passport Size Photo and Mobile Number for completing the online application process.

Q5. Can students apply online?

Yes, eligible students can apply online through the official Bihar Government portal. Students need to fill the application form correctly and upload all required documents.
