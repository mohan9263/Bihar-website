Districts of Bihar

Overview

Bihar is an important state of India with a rich history, cultural heritage and geographical diversity. The state is divided into 38 districts, each having its own unique identity, traditions, historical importance and tourist places. Every district of Bihar contributes to the state's development in areas like agriculture, education, industries, tourism and culture. Districts such as Patna, Gaya, Nalanda, Vaishali and Bhagalpur are famous for their historical and religious importance, while many other districts are known for natural beauty, traditional arts and local industries.

The 38 districts of Bihar are grouped into different divisions for better administration. Each district has its own headquarters, government offices, local culture and famous attractions. From ancient sites like Nalanda University and Mahabodhi Temple to natural places like Valmiki National Park and Kaimur Hills, Bihar's districts offer a wide variety of experiences. These districts represent the diversity of Bihar and play an important role in preserving the state's history, traditions and future development.
