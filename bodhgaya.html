About Bodh Gaya

Bodh Gaya is one of the most sacred pilgrimage destinations for Buddhists around the world. Located in Gaya district of Bihar, it is the place where Siddhartha Gautama attained enlightenment under the Bodhi Tree and became Lord Buddha.

Every year millions of tourists and pilgrims visit Bodh Gaya from countries such as Thailand, Japan, Sri Lanka, China, Myanmar, Bhutan and Nepal.

Historical Importance

The history of Bodh Gaya dates back more than 2500 years. The Mahabodhi Temple Complex was declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2002. The site represents peace, spirituality and Buddhist culture.

Main Attractions

Mahabodhi Temple

UNESCO World Heritage Site and the most important Buddhist temple.

Bodhi Tree

The sacred tree under which Lord Buddha attained enlightenment.

Great Buddha Statue

An 80-feet tall Buddha statue attracting visitors worldwide.

Thai Monastery

Beautiful monastery built in traditional Thai architecture.

Best Time to Visit

October to March is considered the best season to visit Bodh Gaya due to pleasant weather.
