About Nalanda

Nalanda is one of the most famous historical places in Bihar and is known worldwide for the ancient Nalanda University. It was one of the world's first residential universities and attracted students from China, Korea, Tibet, Japan, and many other countries.

The university was established during the Gupta period and became a major center of education, philosophy, astronomy, mathematics, and medicine.

Historical Importance

Nalanda University flourished between the 5th and 12th centuries. Thousands of students and teachers lived and studied here. Historical records indicate that more than 10,000 students and 2,000 teachers were present at the university.

Chinese traveler Xuanzang studied at Nalanda and later wrote detailed descriptions of the institution. His writings provide valuable information about ancient Indian education.
